Zeiss ground almost all of this glass, Schneider and Rodenstock the rest, and Hasselblad sold every bit of it. Filing these under their makers put 44 lenses in the wrong shop — the manufactured-by mistake arrived at from the other direction, because here the *name* carries the maker where the source's own structure does not. The maker stays in each lens's name, where a person reads it, and the address under the maker redirects here.
