The Mamiya 645 Super (1985–1993) is the first of the second-generation bodies. It keeps the modular finder and lens system of the earlier M645 series but introduces removable film magazines that can be changed mid-roll after inserting a dark-slide. Construction is a molded plastic shell over a metal frame.
