The Mamiya 645 1000S (M645 1000S) extends the original M645 with a faster 1/1000 s shutter speed, self-timer, and DoF preview. Manufactured 1976–1990. Retains mirror lock-up and dual shutter releases. Standard lenses included the 80 mm f/2.8 C and 80 mm f/1.9 C.
