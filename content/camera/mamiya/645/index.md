The Mamiya 645 (M645) introduced the 6×4.5 format SLR system in 1975. It is a modular camera with interchangeable prisms (including metered CdS, PD, and AE finders), waist-level finder, and a wide range of Mamiya-Sekor C lenses. Film is loaded in insert cartridges; 15 exposures on 120 film.
