title = "Formats"
+++

The size and cartridge a film was sold in, which is the thing that decides whether it fits a camera at all.

Exposure counts are not formats. A 36-exposure roll and a 24-exposure roll of the same stock are the same film in the same cartridge, so `135-36` and `135-24` are both `135` here — otherwise asking for 35 mm film would miss two thirds of it.
