note = "The K mount plus five electrical contacts, and Pentax's first autofocus attempt. Exactly one body and one lens ever used it: the ME F and the SMC Pentax-AF 35-70mm f/2.8. That answers the question this record used to carry -- it is a distinct mount, not a spelling of the K -- and it also moved the ME Super off this record. The ME Super's infobox reads `[[Pentax K mount|Pentax K-F mount]]`: a link to the K mount wearing the K-F's label, which is what an importer reading labels rather than targets would take. Its own article says the lenses are interchangeable with the K bayonet, and `Pentax K-mount` names it under K twice more."
