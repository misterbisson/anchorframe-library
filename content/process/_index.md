How a film is developed. Most of these are one of three chemistries — C-41 for colour negative, E-6 for slide, and black and white — under a manufacturer's own name.

A film often names both. Fujifilm's CN-16 *is* C-41; Agfa's AP 70 *is* C-41. Each film is filed under every name its process answers to, so asking for C-41 finds the Fujifilm stock too.
